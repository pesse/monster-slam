# Kartenbilder: Access 4 — Wüste

Grundstil, Ablage und Werkbank: [`README.md`](README.md). Jeder Prompt unten ist vollständig
— den ganzen Block kopieren.

## Thema

Weit, abenteuerlich, sonnenverbrannt. Dünen, rote Canyons, trockene Flussbetten, Oasen mit
Palmen, Sandsteinstädte mit Flachdächern und Sonnensegeln. Tiefe Nachmittagssonne, lange
warme Schatten, ein Hauch Hitzeflimmern.

Clean flat colours, soft ambient occlusion, no textures, no painterly brushwork. Mood: adventurous, vast, sun-baked. Light: strong low late-afternoon sun, long warm shadows, faint heat shimmer. The board is a large desert land divided into four clearly separated regions, connected by one winding trail of light path tiles from the lower left to the upper right: 1. lower left: a red-rock canyon coast where the desert meets the sea, with rock arches, wildlife and a small fishing settlement; 2. lower centre: an oasis village with homes around a palm-lined pool, a market and a healer's tent; 3. upper centre: a sandstone workshop town with a school tower, a sports ground and workshops with windmills and water wheels; 4. upper right: an old sandstone capital with a great assembly hall, a forum square with banners and ancient monuments half buried in sand. Each region has a free flat square of empty tiles for a marker. Dunes and canyons separate the regions.
